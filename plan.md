

# Between the Pages — Literary Café Website

A modern, aesthetic single-page website for a literary café in Jaipur with a cozy, warm design using earthy tones.

## Design System
- **Colors**: Earthy browns, warm beige, sage green, cream backgrounds, dark espresso accents
- **Typography**: Elegant serif fonts for headings (Playfair Display), clean sans-serif for body text
- **Style**: Minimalist, cozy, warm — with soft shadows, rounded corners, and generous whitespace

## Sections (Top to Bottom)

### 1. Navigation Bar
- Sticky top nav with logo "Between the Pages"
- Links: Home, Services, Menu, Events, Visit Us
- Highlighted "Reserve a Table" CTA button

### 2. Hero Section
- Full-width warm-toned hero with overlay
- Headline: "More Than a Café. A Place Between the Pages."
- Subheadline about readers, writers, and dreamers
- Two CTA buttons: "Reserve a Table" and "Explore the Menu"

### 3. What We Offer (Services)
- Three visually distinct cards/sections with icons:
  - **Coffee & Beverages** — drink list, student-friendly pricing highlight
  - **Book Library & Reading Space** — 3,000+ books, aesthetic details, quiet zones
  - **Events & Community** — book clubs, poetry nights, open mic, date nights

### 4. Ambience Section
- "A Space Designed to Stay" — immersive, sensory description
- Grid of aesthetic feature highlights (warm interiors, sunlit corners, plants, music)

### 5. Trust / Stats Section
- Dark background with bold statistics: 3,000+ Books, 5 Reading Zones, 8+ Events Monthly, 4.8★ Rating
- Persuasive copy about combining books and coffee

### 6. Menu Section
- Clean tabbed or categorized menu: Coffee, Specialty Drinks, Sides & Snacks
- Items with placeholder prices, student-friendly pricing note

### 7. Made For Section
- Visual cards highlighting each audience segment (college girls, creators, freelancers, writers, couples, solo readers)

### 8. CTA Section
- Emotional headline with three action buttons
- Urgency note about limited weekend tables

### 9. Footer
- Business info, hours (Mon–Sun 9am–9pm), Instagram link, quick links, copyright

## Technical Approach
- Single-page React app with smooth scroll navigation
- All content is static (no backend needed)
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop
- Subtle scroll animations for engagement
